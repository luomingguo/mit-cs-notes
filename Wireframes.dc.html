LEC 01·CSAPP·ch. 2.1–2.3· evergreen updated 2026-04-18·1 open question
Bits, bytes, and integer representation
TL;DR
— Integers are fixed-width bit patterns; arithmetic is modular, not mathematical.
— Two's complement makes subtraction the same circuit as addition, at the cost of an asymmetric range.
— Most C integer bugs are unsigned/signed conversion or overflow, both fully predictable from the encoding.
